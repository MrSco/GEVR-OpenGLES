GEVR Beta vr443
===============

From-source GoldenEye 007 in real OpenXR VR. Bring your own USA ROM.

This zip has no ROM. You supply a USA GoldenEye .z64 you legally own.

Internal cook: 453 (public tag vr443).

This cut is a VR comfort / motion rethink
----------------------------------------
Movement, turning, and the open play space got another pass so the headset
feels more like standing inside the game - not fighting the old flat controls.

What is new in vr443
--------------------
- VR Settings glass on the intro / cinema hub (look right). Right stick:
  up/down picks a row, left/right changes it live.
  - TURN SPEED
  - TURN STYLE (Smooth or Snap)
  - SNAP SIZE (25-150%, grayed out while Smooth)
- Those comfort picks save under %LOCALAPPDATA%\GEVR (with your saves).
  Updating the zip does not wipe them. Clear-GEVR-cache.bat keeps them too.
- Dam sky look-up stays pinned (SKYPIN - on by default).
- In-app Update: open the starter and it checks GitHub Latest for you.
  If a newer cut exists it says so. Click Update to pull the win64 zip into
  this folder. No more hunting the Releases page every time.
  Saves, prefs, cache, and your .z64 beside the exe are left alone.

Play
----
1. Unzip anywhere.
2. Headset: run Start-GEVR.bat
   Monitor / no headset: run Play-on-monitor.bat
3. Point it at your USA .z64.
4. In VR, recenter with both thumbstick clicks.
5. On the intro hub, look right for VR SETTINGS and tune turn comfort.

First time
  The first launch waits once while images prepare into
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\GEVR\cache, then you play.

Updating from an older Beta
  Keep the same .z64. Each cut carries a ship stamp, so the first
  launch after an update rebuilds that cache once automatically.
  Or open Start-GEVR.bat / the starter and use Update when it offers a
  newer tag.
  Your saves and VR Settings prefs stay under %LOCALAPPDATA%\GEVR.

If the picture still looks wrong: prefer Clear-GEVR-cache.bat (keeps saves).
Only delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\GEVR if you are okay losing saves too.

Why older zips
--------------
- vr434 was pulled: ROM images were baked into goldeneye.exe.
- vr438 required your ROM but shipped a stripped VR boot.
- vr440 had the right picture but never turned the chair features on.
- vr441 / vr442 were prior Latest cuts. Grab vr443.

Headset notes
-------------
Auto-Aim defaults OFF. Left stick moves the pause highlight in VR.

In headset, B reloads. Menu / system button opens pause and options.
Tab on keyboard/monitor still works.

Pimax Crystal Super + SteamVR OpenXR, native PimaxXR, Quest 3 +
Virtual Desktop OpenXR.

72 / 80 Hz should work. 90 Hz is recommended (default). Over 90 is
still beta-test territory. Please report issues.
